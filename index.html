CLEAR QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

A little clarity before you start.

Understand the product direction, what is being built and what comes next.

What is a Business OS?+

A Business OS is a connected foundation for the daily work of a business: transactions, stock, relationships and team activity. Stromule uses this term for its direction of bringing operations, business understanding and approved action together, rather than treating billing as an isolated task.

How is Stromule developing inventory management?+

Inventory development includes a product catalogue, stock movements, warehouse records and CSV import. The aim is to connect inventory with sales and purchases. Replenishment recommendations are vision; public availability and end-to-end release readiness are not confirmed.

How could Stromule help retailers and wholesalers?+

For retailers, the intended workflows connect stock, billing and repeat customers. For wholesalers and distributors, the direction includes purchase cycles, supplier relationships and outstanding credit records. These are development use cases, not claims that specialist workflows are released.

What is Stromule?+

Stromule is an Intelligent Business Operating System being developed by Kumpali Private Limited to help everyday businesses run operations, understand activity and act on what needs attention.

Is Stromule an ERP or accounting software?+

It overlaps with familiar business management software, including inventory and billing. Its direction is a connected operating experience spanning daily work, business understanding and approved action. It is not presented as a complete accounting or statutory compliance replacement.

Which businesses can use it?+

The intended audience includes retailers, wholesalers, distributors, small manufacturers, service providers, street vendors and growing businesses. Business-specific workflows remain part of the development direction.

Can I continue using Tally?+

The intended approach is coexistence: keep your accounting system while adopting relevant Stromule workflows. No live Tally integration or automatic data exchange is confirmed.

Can I import my Excel data?+

Inventory CSV import exists in the development app. Preparing Excel records as compatible CSV is the intended starting point. Direct Excel workbook import, general migration and public availability are not confirmed.

Will Stromule work without internet?+

Offline-first operation is in development. Local storage and sync implementations exist, but offline billing, conflict handling and recovery need release validation. Discovery, external payment verification and cloud features require connectivity.

What is the Business Copilot?+

A product vision for answering questions from business records, offering context-aware recommendations, preparing actions and executing supported operations after approval and permission checks. The conversation above is illustrative; a working Copilot is not currently offered on this website.

How does Stromule differ from Pangeabuy?+

Stromule is the operational foundation. Pangeabuy is the Connected Commerce Network for discovery and commerce opportunities. They are intended to work independently, with authorized connections where implemented; the complete connected flow is not confirmed as live.

Will my business information become public?+

The intended policy is business-controlled sharing, not automatic publication of operational records. A future integration should explain what is shared and require appropriate authorization. This informational website does not connect to your business records.

Is Stromule available yet?+

Stromule is in development. Registration is not open on this site, and launch timing and pricing have not been announced. There is no waitlist or payment form.

Will Stromule support international businesses?+

Stromule has a global ambition. India is an important initial market, not the boundary of the product vision. Supported countries, currencies, languages and local requirements for launch remain to be confirmed.
