A FEW SIMPLE ANSWERS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

New to the idea? Start here.

What is Stromule?+

Stromule is a Business Operating System we are building for small businesses. The idea is to give your daily tasks, team, and important details one shared place, so you can understand the work more easily.

Can I use it today?+

Not yet. Stromule is still in development. The examples on this site explain what we are working toward. They are not live product screens.

Is it for a business as small as mine?+

We are thinking about people working on their own as well as small teams. Our aim is to make everyday work easier to understand. Exact features and requirements will be shared before launch.

Will it work in my country and language?+

We are building with small businesses around the world in mind. Supported countries, languages, and local requirements have not been announced yet. We will share those details before the product becomes available.

How much will it cost?+

Pricing has not been announced. This website does not sell a subscription, take payments, or ask for payment details.

Do I need to give you my business information?+

No. You can explore all the examples without an account or personal details. Your choices only change what you see on this page. They are not saved or sent to a Stromule server.
