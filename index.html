OUR APPROACH

Keep the work human.
Give it structure.

Software should help a business make sense of its work. These are the principles we're building around.

A little less friction.
A lot more room to move.
01

Unified by design

+

A task isn't separate from the conversation that started it, or the person taking it forward. We're designing for those relationships from the start.

Context belongs with the work.
02

Built for real work

+

The everyday handoffs, questions, and decisions matter. Our starting point is the practical work of running a business, not a longer list of features.

Start with the everyday.
03

Designed to scale

+

More people and more moving parts shouldn't mean starting over. Our aim is a foundation that can evolve as the business becomes more complex.

Leave room for what comes next.
04

Connected intelligence

+

Information is more useful when you can understand what it relates to. We're thinking about how business information and processes can give each other context.

Make the relationships useful.
