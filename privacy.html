AT A GLANCE

No sign-up form. No advertising trackers. Interactive choices stay in the page. Hosting and external font requests still involve technical information.

1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes the current informational website at stromule.com, published by Kumpali Private Limited under the Stromule name. It covers the website, its interactive illustrations, and its early-access information.

Stromule is in development. This website does not currently offer product accounts, business-data uploads, payments, or a registration form. A future Stromule service may process information differently; its privacy information will be provided before those features are introduced.

2. Information and website requests

You do not need to provide a name, email address, or business information to explore this website. The early-access button opens an informational message; it does not submit your information or add you to a waitlist.

Loading a website still involves technical requests. The hosting and font providers may receive information such as your IP address, browser details, the resources requested, and the time of the request. This information can be used to deliver resources, maintain security, and troubleshoot their services.

3. Cookies and interactive controls

The website's own code does not set cookies or use browser local storage for advertising, analytics, or tracking. It does not include an analytics SDK or an advertising pixel.

Your selections in the relationship map, story tabs, mobile menu, and expandable sections are kept in the current page's memory. They are not submitted to a Stromule server or saved by the site's code after you leave or reload the page.

4. Hosting, fonts, and external links

The website is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. When served there, requests are handled by GitHub's infrastructure. GitHub's handling of technical request information is described in its Privacy Statement.

The website requests Poppins and Aref Ruqaa Ink through Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's font servers to retrieve the styles and font files. See Google Fonts' FAQ and Google's Privacy Policy for information about that service.

If you follow an external link, the destination website's own policies apply. Stromule's website does not control those websites or their data practices.

5. How information is used and shared

The current site does not collect form submissions, maintain a visitor-profile database, sell personal information, or share visitor profiles for advertising. The technical requests described above are necessary to load the website and its fonts.

Service providers handle information under their own policies and may process it in countries other than your own. This policy does not set or override their retention periods, security practices, or legal obligations.

6. Retention and security

Because the current website has no registration or submission backend, it does not maintain a Stromule database of sign-ups or business records. Interactive choices in the page are temporary.

Hosting and font providers determine retention of their technical records under their own policies. No internet transmission or hosting system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not try to submit sensitive business or personal information through this informational site.

7. Your choices and questions

You can control requests and browser storage through your browser settings or available privacy tools. Blocking external fonts may change how text is displayed. The website does not require an account to read its content.

Depending on where you live and the processing involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the use of personal information. For information processed independently by a hosting or font provider, use the privacy request channels in that provider's policy.

A dedicated Stromule privacy contact has not yet been published. This page will be updated with a verified contact channel before the site begins collecting registrations or other submissions.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy describes the website as it is currently implemented. If we introduce registration, analytics, new providers, or other data-handling features, we will update this page to explain them and obtain consent where required.

The date at the top of this page identifies the latest update.
